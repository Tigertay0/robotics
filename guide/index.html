Step 1

    Do 3D-printed parts help?

    The rules allow PLA or PETG prints in grey only: 6 small parts (60 × 60 × 20 mm), 2 mid-size (220 × 220 × 80 mm) and 1 large (220 × 220 × 250 mm), with every STL submitted before the tournament. Prints don't score by themselves. They matter where a part's shape decides whether a high-value item works. Ranked by the points they protect:

    PrintSize classReplacesWhyPoints at stake (×5)

    Claw pads shaped for a 1.6" pom2 smallbare liftarm fingersA pom slips out of flat fingers; the red pom is grabbed once and is worth 250.255
    Cup hook with a lip for the 5.1" rim1 smallbent 1×10 strapCatches the rim at any approach angle.210

    Ring fork, 200 mm1 largenothing exists yetLifts a stack of 3 rings off a post to drop over a bottle neck (15 each, tiebreaker #1). Needs a ~9" lift the servo arm can't give: a new mechanism, not just a print.up to 480

    Order: print the lane first (it is the only print that touches the multiplier), then the claw pads and the cup hook. Leave the ring fork until the core plan scores 500+ three times in a row. Full write-up in 3D_PRINTS.md.
